# Clinical Scope

## In Scope

- Constitutional CNV
- Copy-number loss
- Copy-number gain
- Postnatal/prenatal educational context
- Structured interpretation support
- ACMG/ClinGen Riggs et al. 2020 framework
- Turkish clinician education

## Out of Scope

- Acquired CNVs in neoplasia
- Somatic CNVs
- Validated routine clinical reporting
- Medical device claims
- Automatic diagnosis
- Replacing expert review
- Automated clinical decision-making
- Unvalidated patient-specific treatment recommendations
